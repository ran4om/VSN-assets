# 6. English Vocabulary: Enquiry and Offer

## 6.1 Essential Business Vocabulary

💡 **KEY POINT:** Essential English vocabulary for international trade based on the provided worksheets.

### 1. Parties to the Contract / Beteiligte am Kaufvertrag
* **buyer:** The person/company purchasing goods. (Käufer)
* **seller:** The person/company offering and selling goods. (Verkäufer)

### 2. Enquiry and Offer / Anfrage und Angebot
* **enquiry:** A request for information. (Anfrage)
* **offer:** A proposal to sell goods under specific terms. (Angebot)
* **unsolicited offer:** An offer made without a prior inquiry. (unverlangtes Angebot)
* **demanded offer:** An offer made in response to an inquiry. (verlangtes Angebot)
* **counteroffer:** A response proposing changes to the original offer. (Gegenangebot)
* **unaddressed mailing:** Mass mailings not targeting a specific person. (Postwurfsendung)

### 3. Terms and Conditions / Bedingungen
* **terms of delivery:** Conditions detailing how goods are shipped. (Lieferbedingungen)
* **terms of payment:** Conditions detailing how and when to pay. (Zahlungsbedingungen)

### 4. Conclusion and Legal Effects / Vertragsabschluss
* **conclusion of a sales contract:** When buyer and seller agree to terms. (Abschluss eines Kaufvertrags)
* **binding / not binding:** Whether an offer carries a legal obligation. (verbindlich / unverbindlich)
* **legal binding period:** The time frame during which an offer is valid. (gesetzliche Bindungsfrist)
* **provisional offer:** A non-binding offer. (freibleibendes Angebot)

### 5. Clauses and Other Terms / Sonstige Begriffe
* **stocks:** Inventory available. (Vorräte, Lagerbestand)
* **"Only while stocks last":** Clause limiting availability. (Nur solange der Vorrat reicht)
* **"Prices are subject to change without notice":** Clause protecting seller from price fluctuations. (Preisänderungen vorbehalten)
* **delivery time:** Time taken to ship goods. (Lieferzeit)
* **consideration period:** Time given to the buyer to think about the offer. (Überlegungsfrist)

## 6.2 Key Logical Concepts (Fill-in-the-gaps Logic)

⚡ **EXAM TIP:** Summary of the logical flow used in English business correspondence. Make sure you understand the sequence of these bolded terms.

* **The Enquiry Process:** The **buyer** asks the **seller** to make an offer. If the buyer wants to change conditions, they write a **counteroffer**. An enquiry generally concerns prices, **terms of delivery or payment**, or technical descriptions.
* **The Offer Process:** An offer is made by the **seller** and should lead to the **conclusion of a sales contract**. 
* **Binding Rules:** A **provisional offer** is not **legal/binding** to the seller. Clauses like "Only while **stocks** last" are used. Unaddressed mailings are never binding.
* **Time limits:** Under people present, there is no **consideration period**. For absent people, **double** delivery time and an appropriate consideration period are applied.
