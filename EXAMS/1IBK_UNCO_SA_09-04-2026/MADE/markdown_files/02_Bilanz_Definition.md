# Die Bilanz - Definition & Struktur | Balance Sheet - Definition & Structure

> **DEFINITION:** The Balance Sheet (Bilanz) is a statement that shows the assets and capital of a company at a specific date (balance sheet date/Bilanzstichtag).

### English

- **Assets (Aktiva)** represent what the company owns
- **Capital & Liabilities (Passiva)** represent how the assets are financed

### Deutsch

- **Aktiva (Vermögen)** zeigen, was das Unternehmen besitzt
- **Passiva (Kapital)** zeigen, wie das Vermögen finanziert wird

---

## Struktur der Bilanz | Structure of the Balance Sheet

### AKTIVA (Assets) - Left Side

| Category | Examples | Examples (DE) |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| **Anlagevermögen** (Fixed Assets) | Immaterial Assets, Tangible Assets, Financial Assets | Immaterielle VG, Sachanlagen, Finanzanlagen |
| **Umlaufvermögen** (Current Assets) | Inventory, Accounts Receivable, Cash at Bank, Cash at Hand | Vorräte, Forderungen, Bankguthaben, Kassenbestand |

### PASSIVA (Capital & Liabilities) - Right Side

| Category | Examples | Examples (DE) |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| **Eigenkapital** (Equity) | Owner's capital, Net Worth | Eigenkapital, Jahresüberschuss |
| **Fremdkapital** (Liabilities) | Bank Loans, Accounts Payable, Tax Liabilities | Darlehen, Lieferverbindlichkeiten, Umsatzsteuer |

---

## 💡 KEY POINT: Aktiva = Passiva

> Remember: The balance sheet must always balance!
> 
> **AKTIVA = EIGENKAPITAL + FREMDKAPITAL**

---

## ⚡ EXAM TIP

When asked to define the balance sheet, remember these key elements:

1. It shows the financial position at a specific date (stichtagsbezogen)
2. It consists of Aktiva (left) and Passiva (right)
3. It must always balance (Bilanzgleichung)
