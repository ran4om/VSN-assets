# Bruttogewinn | Gross Profit

> **DEFINITION:** Gross Profit is the difference between net sales revenue and cost of goods sold. It shows the profitability of core business (buying and selling) before operating expenses.

---

## The Formula | Die Formel

### English

```
Gross Profit = Sales Revenues - Cost of Goods Sold
```

### Deutsch

```
Bruttogewinn = Handelswarenerlöse - Handelswareneinsatz
```

---

## Example | Beispiel

### English

**Given:**
- Sales Revenues: €15,000
- Cost of Goods Sold: €10,000

**Calculation:**
```
Gross Profit = €15,000 - €10,000 = €5,000
```

**Result:** €5,000 Profit

### Deutsch

**Gegeben:**
- Handelswarenerlöse: €15,000
- Handelswareneinsatz: €10,000

**Berechnung:**
```
Bruttogewinn = €15.000 - €10.000 = €5.000
```

**Ergebnis:** €5.000 Gewinn

---

## Gross Profit Margin | Bruttogewinnmarge

### English

```
Gross Profit Margin = (Gross Profit ÷ Sales) × 100
```

**In our example:**
```
(€5,000 ÷ €15,000) × 100 = 33.33%
```

### Deutsch

```
Bruttogewinnmarge = (Bruttogewinn ÷ Erlöse) × 100
```

**Im Beispiel:**
```
(€5.000 ÷ €15.000) × 100 = 33,33%
```

---

## 💡 KEY POINT

> Gross profit shows how efficiently a company buys and sells goods. A higher gross profit margin indicates better profitability in the core business!

---

## ⚡ EXAM TIP

The gross profit calculation is fundamental:

- It's the first step in determining net profit
- It excludes operating expenses, interest, and taxes
- The margin percentage is often used to compare companies

**Formula to remember:**
```
Bruttogewinn = Erlöse - Einsatz
```
