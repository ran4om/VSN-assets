## 3 Merkmale der Doppelten Buchführung | 3 Characteristics of Double-Entry Bookkeeping

> **DEFINITION:** The double-entry bookkeeping system ensures that every business transaction affects at least two accounts, with equal amounts in debit (Soll) and credit (Haben).

### English

1. **Dual Entry for Every Amount:** Every amount is posted twice - once in debit (Soll) and once in credit (Haben).

2. **Dual Entry for Every Business Transaction:** Every transaction is recorded in the general ledger (Hauptbuch - all accounts) and the journal (Journal - chronological order).

3. **Dual Profit Determination:** Profit is determined directly (Income Statement/G&V) and indirectly (comparison of assets - Eigenkapital).

### Deutsch

1. **2-Fache Verbuchung jedes Betrages:** Jeder Betrag wird zweimal verbucht - einmal im Soll und einmal im Haben.

2. **2-Fache Erfassung jedes Geschäftsfalles:** Jeder Geschäftsfall wird im Hauptbuch (alle Konten) und im Journal (chronologische Reihenfolge) erfasst.

3. **2-Fache Erfolgsermittlung:** Der Erfolg wird direkt (GuV) und indirekt (Vergleich des Eigenkapitals) ermittelt.

---

## 💡 KEY POINT: The Accounting Equation

> **DEFINITION:** The fundamental accounting equation is:
> 
> **AKTIVA = EIGENKAPITAL + FREMDKAPITAL**
> 
> (Assets = Equity + Liabilities)

This must always balance - the total on the left side must equal the total on the right side.

---

## ⚡ EXAM TIP

Remember the three characteristics of double-entry bookkeeping. This is a common exam question!

- Type 1 asks: "What are the 3 characteristics of double-entry bookkeeping?"
- Answer: Dual entry for every amount, every transaction, and dual profit determination
