## Question 1: Net Price Calculation

### English

A laptop costs €1,450 including 20% VAT. What is the net price (without VAT)?

### Deutsch

Ein Laptop kostet €1.450 inklusive 20% MWSt. Was ist der Nettopreis (ohne MWSt)?

---

### ✅ Answer

**€1,208.33**

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Understanding the problem:**
- Gross price (€1,450) = 120% of the net price
- (100% + 20% VAT = 120%)

**Solution:**

1. **Let G = net price** (what we're looking for)

2. **Set up the equation:**
   ```
   G × 1.20 = €1,450
   ```

3. **Solve for G:**
   ```
   G = €1,450 ÷ 1.20
   G = €1,208.33
   ```

---

> **💡 KEY POINT:** To remove VAT (add 20%), we divide by 1.20. To add VAT, we multiply by 1.20. The "auf" factor directly gives us the calculation method.

## Question 2: Percentage Decrease

### English

A car's value decreases from €25,000 to €18,500. What is the percentage decrease?

### Deutsch

Der Wert eines Autos sinkt von €25.000 auf €18.500. Wie hoch ist die prozentuale Abnahme?

---

### ✅ Answer

**26% decrease**

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **Calculate the decrease:**
   ```
   €25,000 - €18,500 = €6,500
   ```

2. **Find what percentage €6,500 is of the original €25,000:**
   ```
   Percentage = (Decrease ÷ Original) × 100
   Percentage = (€6,500 ÷ €25,000) × 100
   Percentage = 0.26 × 100
   Percentage = 26%
   ```

---

> **⚡ EXAM TIP:** Always divide by the ORIGINAL value (before the change), not the new value!

## Question 3: Sequential Discounts

### English

Shop A offers 20% discount, then an additional 15% off the sale price. Shop B offers 35% discount on the original price. Which shop is better?

### Deutsch

Geschäft A bietet 20% Rabatt, dann zusätzlich 15% auf den Sale-Preis. Geschäft B bietet 35% Rabatt auf den Originalpreis. Welches Geschäft ist besser?

---

### ✅ Answer

**Shop A is better** (pays 68% vs 65% of original)

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Shop A (sequential discounts):**

1. **First discount:** 100% - 20% = **80%** remaining
2. **Second discount:** 80% × (100% - 15%) = 80% × 85% = **68%** of original

**Shop B (single discount):**

1. **Total discount:** 100% - 35% = **65%** of original

**Comparison:**
- Shop A: Pay **68%** of original price
- Shop B: Pay **65%** of original price
- **Shop A is better!**

---

> **💡 KEY POINT:** Sequential discounts are calculated on the REDUCED amount, not the original. This is why Shop A ends up being more expensive than a single 35% discount!

## Question 4: Percentage Points vs. Percentage Change

### English

An interest rate changes from 4% to 5%. Express this as:
a) Percentage points
b) Percentage change

### Deutsch

Ein Zinssatz ändert sich von 4% auf 5%. Drücken Sie dies aus als:
a) Prozentpunkte
b) Prozentuale Änderung

---

### ✅ Answer

(a) **1 percentage point** (b) **25% increase**

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Breakdown

**(a) Percentage points:**
```
5% - 4% = 1 percentage point
```

**(b) Percentage change:**
```
(New - Old) ÷ Old × 100
= (5% - 4%) ÷ 4% × 100
= 1% ÷ 4% × 100
= 0.25 × 100
= 25%
```

---

> **⚡ EXAM TIP:** 
> - "Percentage points" = the simple subtraction (absolute difference)
> - "Percentage change" = relative to the original (what percentage bigger it is now)
