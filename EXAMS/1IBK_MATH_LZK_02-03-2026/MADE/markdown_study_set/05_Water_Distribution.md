## 5.1 Overview

### English

The following data illustrates the total water distribution on Earth. Understanding this data helps practice percentage calculations with real-world context.

**Key Facts:**
- **Global Water Volume:** Earth has a total of 1,317,398,571 km³ of seawater
- **Saltwater:** 97% of all water
- **Freshwater:** Only 3% of all water
- **Usable Water:** Only surface water and groundwater are usable for drinking water without excessive energy input

## 💡 KEY POINT: Why This Matters

Notice that only **0.3%** of all water is surface water (lakes, rivers, swamps), and of that tiny amount, **87%** is in lakes.

This means lakes represent only about **0.26%** of all Earth's water!

This tiny percentage explains why freshwater is such a precious resource.

## 🔢 Practice Calculations

### Calculation 1: What percentage of ALL water is freshwater surface water?

**Step-by-step:**
1. Freshwater = 3% = 0.03
2. Surface water (of freshwater) = 0.3% = 0.003
3. Calculation: 0.03 × 0.003 = 0.0000783
4. As percentage: 0.0000783 × 100 = **0.00783%**

### Calculation 2: What percentage of ALL water are lakes?

**Step-by-step:**
1. Freshwater = 3% = 0.03
2. Surface water = 0.3% = 0.003
3. Lakes (of surface water) = 87% = 0.87
4. Calculation: 0.03 × 0.003 × 0.87 = 0.0000783
5. As percentage: **0.00783%**

## ⚡ EXAM TIP: Multi-Step Percentage Problems

> When calculating what percentage one value is of another through multiple steps:
> 
> **Multiply all the decimal values together**, then convert to percentage.

**Formula:**
```
Total % = (Percentage1 × Percentage2 × Percentage3...)
```

**Example:** What % of total water are lakes?
- 3% (freshwater) × 0.3% (surface water) × 87% (lakes)
- = 0.03 × 0.003 × 0.87 = 0.0000783
- = 0.00783%
