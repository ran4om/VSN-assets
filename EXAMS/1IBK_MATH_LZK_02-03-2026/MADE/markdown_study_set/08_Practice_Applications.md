## Question 1: Increase Then Decrease

### English

Sarah buys a computer for €1,600. The price first increases by 10%, then decreases by 10%. What is the final price?

### Deutsch

Sarah kauft einen Computer für €1.600. Der Preis erhöht sich zuerst um 10%, dann sinkt er um 10%. Was ist der Endpreis?

---

### ✅ Answer

**€1,584**

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **Increase by 10%:**
   ```
   €1,600 × 1.10 = €1,760
   ```

2. **Then decrease by 10%:**
   ```
   €1,760 × 0.90 = €1,584
   ```

---

> **⚡ EXAM TIP: Why This Is Tricky!**
> 
> The 10% decrease is calculated on €1,760 (the INCREASED amount), not the original €1,600!
> 
> This is why a 10% increase followed by a 10% decrease does NOT get you back to the original!

---

### Quick Method: Multiply Both Factors

```
1.10 × 0.90 = 0.99
Final = €1,600 × 0.99 = €1,584
```

> **💡 KEY POINT:** A 10% increase followed by a 10% decrease leaves you at 99% — you lose 1%!

## Question 2: Election Results Analysis

### English

At an election, Party A gained 45% of votes (up from 36% last time). 
a) How many percentage points did they gain?
b) What was their percentage increase?

### Deutsch

Bei einer Wahl erhielt Partei A 45% der Stimmen (vorher 36%).
a) Wie viele Prozentpunkte gewannen sie?
b) Wie hoch war die prozentuale Steigerung?

---

### ✅ Answer

(a) **9 percentage points** (b) **25% increase**

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Breakdown

**(a) Percentage points (absolute difference):**
```
45% - 36% = 9 percentage points
```

**(b) Percentage change (relative to original):**
```
(45% - 36%) ÷ 36% × 100
= 9% ÷ 36% × 100
= 0.25 × 100
= 25%
```

---

> **⚡ EXAM TIP:** Both numbers are useful:
> - "9 percentage points gained" tells you the absolute difference in the score
> - "25% increase" tells you the relative growth from their previous position

## Question 3: Water Distribution Calculation

### English

Using the water distribution data, calculate what percentage of ALL water on Earth is surface water (lakes + swamps + rivers)?

### Deutsch

Berechnen Sie mit den Wasserverteilungsdaten, wie viel Prozent des GESAMTEN Wassers Oberflächenwasser ist (Seen + Sümpfe + Flüsse)?

---

### ✅ Answer

**0.00783%**

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Breakdown

From the water distribution data:
- Saltwater: 97% = 0.97
- Freshwater: 3% = 0.03
- Surface water (of freshwater): 0.3% = 0.003

**Calculation:**
```
0.03 × 0.003 = 0.0000783
As percentage: 0.0000783 × 100 = 0.00783%
```

---

> **💡 KEY POINT:** This tiny percentage explains why freshwater is such a precious resource!

## Question 4: Salary Negotiation Models

### English

Compare two salary models:
- **Model A:** Starting salary €2,000/month, +€50/month each year
- **Model B:** Starting salary €2,222/month, +1.5% each year

After 4 years, which model gives higher pay?

### Deutsch

Vergleichen Sie zwei Gehaltsmodelle:
- **Modell A:** Startgehalt €2.000/Monat, +€50/Monat pro Jahr
- **Modell B:** Startgehalt €2.222/Monat, +1,5% pro Jahr

Nach 4 Jahren, welches Modell gibt mehr Gehalt?

---

### ✅ Answer

**Model B is better** (€2,356.33 vs €2,200)

---

### 📝 Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Model A (fixed increase):**
```
Year 1: €2,000
Year 2: €2,000 + €50 = €2,050
Year 3: €2,050 + €50 = €2,100
Year 4: €2,100 + €50 = €2,150
```

Wait, that's monthly! After 4 years in the company (entering year 4):
```
Starting: €2,000
After 3 years: €2,000 + (3 × €50) = €2,150
```

---

**Model B (percentage increase):**
```
Year 1: €2,222
Year 2: €2,222 × 1.015 = €2,255.33
Year 3: €2,255.33 × 1.015 = €2,289.16
Year 4: €2,289.16 × 1.015 = €2,323.50
```

After 4 years in company (entering year 4, completed 3 raises):
```
Starting: €2,222
After 3 years: €2,222 × 1.015³ = €2,356.33
```

---

**Comparison after 4 years:**
- Model A: €2,200 (or €2,150 if still in year 4)
- Model B: €2,356.33
- **Difference: €156.33 more with Model B**

---

> **⚡ EXAM TIP:** For long-term planning, percentage-based raises often outpace fixed increases, especially as your starting salary gets higher!
