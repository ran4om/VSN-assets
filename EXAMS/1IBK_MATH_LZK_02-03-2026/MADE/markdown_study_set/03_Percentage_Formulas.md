## 📝 Definition: Key Components

> **DEFINITION:** The core of percentage calculation involves three main components:

| Symbol | German | English | Description |
|--------|--------|---------|-------------|
| **A** | Anteil | Part/Share | The value *after* a change or the specific part of the base value |
| **G** | Grundwert | Base Value | The original value or the total amount *before* a change |
| **P** | Prozentsatz | Percentage Rate | The percentage expressed as a decimal (e.g., 20% = 0.2) |

> **🔢 CORE FORMULA:** **A = G × P**

---

## 3.1 Calculating the Part (Anteilberechnung)

### English

**Goal:** Find A when G and P are known.

**Formula:** A = G × P

**Example Problem:** A fruit farmer harvested 960 kg of apples, 80% of which he sold in his farm shop. How many kg of apples did he sell?

- **Given:** G = 960 kg, P = 80% = 0.8
- **Wanted:** A (Anteil)
- **Calculation:** A = 960 × 0.8 = **768 kg**
- **Result:** He sold 768 kg of apples.

### Deutsch

**Ziel:** Den Anteil (A) finden, wenn G und P bekannt sind.

**Formel:** A = G × P

**Beispielaufgabe:** Ein Obstbauer hat 960 kg Äpfel geerntet, von denen er 80% in seinem Hofladen verkauft hat.

- **Gegeben:** G = 960 kg, P = 80% = 0.8
- **Gesucht:** A (Anteil)
- **Berechnung:** A = 960 × 0.8 = **768 kg**
- **Ergebnis:** Er hat 768 kg Äpfel verkauft.

---

## 3.2 Calculating the Base Value (Grundwertberechnung)

### English

**Goal:** Find G when A and P are known.

**Formula:** G = A ÷ P

**Example Problem:** 5 students in a class are sick today, which represents 20% of all students in the class. Calculate the total number of students.

- **Given:** A = 5 students, P = 20% = 0.2
- **Wanted:** G (Grundwert)
- **Calculation:** G = 5 ÷ 0.2 = **25 students**
- **Result:** There are 25 students in the class.

### Deutsch

**Ziel:** Den Grundwert (G) finden, wenn A und P bekannt sind.

**Formel:** G = A ÷ P

**Beispielaufgabe:** 5 Schüler einer Klasse sind heute krank, das sind 20% aller Schüler.

- **Gegeben:** A = 5 Schüler, P = 20% = 0.2
- **Gesucht:** G (Grundwert)
- **Berechnung:** G = 5 ÷ 0.2 = **25 Schüler**
- **Ergebnis:** Es sind 25 Schüler in der Klasse.

---

## 3.3 Calculating the Percentage Rate (Prozentsatzberechnung)

### English

**Goal:** Find P when A and G are known.

**Formula:** P = A ÷ G

**Example Problem:** Of 25 students, 8 forgot their homework today. Calculate what percentage this is.

- **Given:** G = 25 students, A = 8 students
- **Wanted:** P (Prozentsatz)
- **Calculation:** P = 8 ÷ 25 = 0.32 = **32%**
- **Result:** 32% of the students forgot their homework.

### Deutsch

**Ziel:** Den Prozentsatz (P) finden, wenn A und G bekannt sind.

**Formel:** P = A ÷ G

**Beispielaufgabe:** Von 25 Schülern haben heute 8 ihre Hausaufgaben vergessen.

- **Gegeben:** G = 25 Schüler, A = 8 Schüler
- **Gesucht:** P (Prozentsatz)
- **Berechnung:** P = 8 ÷ 25 = 0.32 = **32%**
- **Ergebnis:** 32% der Schüler haben vergessen.

---

## 💡 Quick Reference: Which Formula to Use?

| What do you need? | Use this formula |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Find the part (A) | A = G × P |
| Find the base (G) | G = A ÷ P |
| Find the rate (P) | P = A ÷ G |

---

> **💡 KEY POINT:** Remember: A is always the SMALLER number (part of the whole), G is the ORIGINAL number (before change), and P is the PERCENTAGE (as a decimal).
