## Introduction

### 🇬🇧 English

💡 **KEY POINT:** The living conditions on Earth vary significantly between the poles and the equator. This is primarily due to the differing **angle of incidence** of sunlight, but also other ecological factors.

**Ecological Factors Creating Variation:**
- 🌍 Warm or cold ocean currents off the coast
- 🌍 Mountain ranges where moist air masses accumulate on the **windward side** (Luv)
- 🌍 Dryness in the **rain shadow** on the leeward side (Lee)

This creates a very complex picture of Earth's landscape zones.

### 🇩🇪 Deutsch

💡 **KEY POINT:** Die Lebensbedingungen auf der Erde sind zwischen den Polen und dem Äquator äußerst unterschiedlich. Das liegt in erster Linie am unterschiedlichen **Einfallswinkel** der Sonnenstrahlen, aber auch an anderen ökologischen Faktoren.

**Ökologische Faktoren, die Variation schaffen:**
- 🌍 Warme oder kalte Meeresströmungen vor der Küste
- 🌍 Gebirge, in deren **Luv** sich feuchte Luftmassen stauen
- 🌍 Trockenheit im **Regenschatten** (im Lee)

Dadurch ergibt sich ein äußerst vielschichtiges Bild.

## Water Balance Definitions

> **🔑 DEFINITION: Arid (dry, barren)**
> 
> In an arid climate, **evaporation exceeds precipitation**.
> 
> **Distinction:**
> - **Vollarid** = dry all year
> - **Semiarid** = dry during part of the year

> **🔑 DEFINITION: Humid (moist)**
> 
> In a humid climate, **precipitation exceeds evaporation**.
> 
> **Distinction:**
> - **Vollhumid** = sufficient precipitation all year
> - **Semihumid** = sufficient precipitation during part of the year

⚡ **EXAM TIP:** Remember the simple rule:
- **Arid**: Evaporation > Precipitation (DRY)
- **Humid**: Precipitation > Evaporation (MOIST)

## Landscape Zones Classification Table

📊 **COMPREHENSIVE LANDSCAPE ZONE TABLE**

| Landscape Zone | Temperature Zone | Seasons | Water Balance |
|---------------|------------------|---------|---------------|
| **Rainforest** | Tropical | Not pronounced (25°C year-round) | **Humid** (high precipitation) |
| **Savanna** | Tropical | Slightly pronounced | **Humid/Arid alternating** |
| **Desert / Semi-desert** | Subtropical | Strongly pronounced | **Arid** (dry year-round) |
| **Mediterranean** | Subtropical | Hot summers, mild winters | Summer **arid**, winter **humid** |
| **Deciduous / Mixed Forests** | Temperate | Moderately pronounced | **Humid** (precipitation year-round) |
| **Steppes** | Temperate | Strongly pronounced | Predominantly **arid** |
| **Boreal Forests / Taiga** | Subpolar | Strongly pronounced | **Humid** (snow in winter) |
| **Tundra** | Subpolar | Strongly pronounced | **Humid** (low precipitation, long winters) |
| **Cold Desert** | Polar | Slightly pronounced | Year-round snow-covered |

⚡ **EXAM TIP:** Focus on memorizing the water balance column — it's frequently tested!

💡 **KEY POINT:** Notice the pattern:
- **Tropical & Subtropical zones** → Temperature determines seasons
- **Temperate & Subpolar zones** → Distinct seasonal changes
- **Water balance** varies from humid (rainforests) to arid (deserts)

## 🌍 Geographic Distribution Patterns

### Tropical Zones (Near Equator)
- **Rainforest**: Year-round warmth and abundant rain
- **Savanna**: Alternating wet and dry seasons

### Subtropical Zones
- **Deserts**: Hot, dry year-round (e.g., Sahara)
- **Mediterranean**: Dry summers, wet winters (e.g., Southern Europe)

### Temperate Zones
- **Deciduous Forests**: Four distinct seasons, year-round precipitation (e.g., Central Europe)
- **Steppes**: Continental, dry grasslands (e.g., Mongolia, Great Plains)

### Subpolar Zones
- **Taiga**: Cold coniferous forests (e.g., Siberia, Canada)
- **Tundra**: Treeless, frozen ground (e.g., Northern Russia, Alaska)

### Polar Zones
- **Cold Desert**: Permanent ice (Antarctica, Greenland)
