## 📚 Complete File Structure

This study set consists of **7 files total** (including this master guide):

### **File 01: Climate Change and Its Consequences**
**Filename:** `01_Climate_Change.md`

**Topics Covered:**
- Global temperature increase (0.6°C in 20th century)
- Glacier retreat and water scarcity
- Arctic ice cover thinning
- Polar ice sheet retreat
- Sea level rise (20 cm in 20th century, projected 2 m by 2100)
- Ocean current changes (North Atlantic Current)
- Increase in extreme weather events
- Worldwide CO₂ emissions data
- GEO-TRAINING exercises

**Key Learning Points:** 7 major climate change consequences, CO₂ emission patterns by country

---

### **File 02: Ecological Classification of Earth's Landscapes**
**Filename:** `02_Ecological_Classification.md`

**Topics Covered:**
- Living conditions from poles to equator
- Ecological factors (sunlight angle, ocean currents, mountain ranges)
- Water balance definitions (Arid vs. Humid)
- Landscape zone classification table
- Temperature zones and seasonal patterns

**Key Learning Points:** Arid/humid definitions, landscape zone characteristics table

---

### **File 03: Tropical Rainforests - Destruction and Causes**
**Filename:** `03_Tropical_Rainforests.md`

**Topics Covered:**
- Biodiversity facts (3/4 of all species)
- Destruction rate (football field per second)
- Causes of exploitation:
  - Export of tropical timber
  - Cash crops and monocultures
  - Mineral resources
  - Population growth
  - Cattle ranching

**Key Learning Points:** Destruction statistics, 5 major exploitation causes

---

### **File 04: Desertification**
**Filename:** `04_Desertification.md`

**Topics Covered:**
- Definition of desertification
- Human causes (over-use, mis-use, inappropriate practices)
- Progressive spread of deserts

**Key Learning Points:** Clear definition, human impact factors

---

### **File 05: Steppes**
**Filename:** `05_Steppes.md`

**Topics Covered:**
- Continental steppe characteristics
- Precipitation patterns and drought periods
- Grass-dominated vegetation (no trees)
- Fire and animal husbandry as formation factors
- Regional examples (Pampas, Great Plains)
- Common misconceptions

**Key Learning Points:** Steppe vs. desert comparison, regional terminology, exam tips

---

### **File 06: Subpolar and Polar Zones**
**Filename:** `06_Polar_Zones.md`

**Topics Covered:**
- Boreal, Taiga, and Tundra definitions
- Climate change impacts on polar regions
- Northeast Passage (Northern Sea Route) opportunities
- Polar regions comparison table (Antarctic vs. Arctic)
- Competence check learning objectives

**Key Learning Points:** Polar terminology, Northeast Passage economics, polar comparison
