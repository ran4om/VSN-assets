# Master Study Guide - Bestandteile des Kaufvertrags

## Major Points of a Sales Contract

---

### Overview

This comprehensive study guide covers all essential components of a sales contract (Kaufvertrag). The content is organized into modular files covering legally required elements, commercial terms, and other contractual components.

### Key Topics Summary

**Legally Required Components (Gesetzliche Bestandteile):**
- Buyer and Seller identification
- Quality (Qualität)
- Quantity (Quantität)
- Price (Preis)

**Commercial Components (Kaufmännische Bestandteile):**
- Delivery Terms (Lieferbedingungen)
- Payment Terms (Zahlungsbedingungen)

**Other Components (Sonstige Bestandteile):**
- Eigentumsvorbehalt (Reservation of Ownership)
- Pönale (Penalty Clause)
- AGB (General Terms and Conditions)
- Packaging (Verpackung)
